MedPlace Medical Supplies & Pharma sells medical equipment and supplies at friendly prices, to hospitals, clinics and ordinary families, with Lazada and Shopee as buying channels. The look should feel like a budget-minded brand you can relate to: honest, upbeat, easy to afford, never fancy or cold.

## Content fundamentals

- Talk like a helpful neighbor who knows medical equipment. Plain words, short lines, no jargon unless the product name needs it.
- Lead with the product, then the price, then how to buy. Real banner lines to reuse: "Your #1 Provider", "Medical Supplies & Equipment", "Affordable medical supplies trusted by hospitals nationwide!".
- Name products exactly as sold, in Title Case, such as Littmann Stethoscope or Proctoscope Anoscope Surgical Kit. The page wraps product names in lightning bolts; keep that habit only in captions and never on images.
- Say "Affordable" and show the price. Prices use `price-xl` in `orange-deep`; add FROM in `caption` when a range applies.
- Taglish and everyday phrases are welcome in captions ("Message us po"). Keep banner headlines in English.
- One call to action per piece: Message us or Order on Lazada or Shopee. List the number and email once, at the bottom.
- Never imply a medical claim beyond what the product box says.

## Visual foundations

- **Color.** Ground is `surface` (warm white). Product photos sit on `surface-raised`. `orange` is the logo orange: use it for the main button and big shapes with `on-orange` text, never white. `teal` is the logo green: shapes and wave backgrounds only. Text colors are `ink`, `ink-muted`, `orange-deep` (prices) and `teal-deep` (links, secondary buttons). `sale` is reserved for a SALE sticker and always shows the word.
- **Type.** Bricolage Grotesque for display (`price-xl`, `display`, `heading`, `subheading`), DM Sans for everything else. Bold and big for prices, calm for specs.
- **Shape.** Friendly but firm: buttons and product windows use `radius-md`, cards `radius-lg`, stickers and chips `radius-pill`. The banner uses soft teal waves and a rounded-diamond photo frame; reuse a wave for section dividers.
- **Photos.** Clean product cut-outs on white, centered, large. Show the real item, never a stock lookalike. No heavy filters.
- **Depth.** Flat and sticker-like: `shadow-flat` under buttons and sale tags, `shadow-card` on cards. No glass or glow.
- **Space.** Compact but not cramped: `space-4` inside cards, `space-6` between sections.
- **Motion.** Quick and light: 150ms press-down on buttons. Nothing bounces.
- **Focus.** `focus` ring, 3px solid, 3px offset.
- **Night theme.** Same names, warm dark ground, for screens at night.

## Product post anatomy

Matches the page's plain product photos, with a price added:

1. `surface-raised` window (`radius-md`) with the product cut-out.
2. Product name in `subheading`, `ink`.
3. Price in `price-xl`, `orange-deep`, with FROM in `caption` if needed.
4. Optional `sale` sticker (`radius-pill`) with the percent off.
5. A `orange` button reading Message us, and small Lazada and Shopee chips.
