Emmanuelle Pharma & Medical Supplies is a Philippine medical supplier serving home and corporate buyers, wholesale and retail. The look must feel relaxing and soothing: a quiet, trustworthy presence that lowers the reader's shoulders. It never shouts.

## Content fundamentals

- Write warm, plain and reassuring, the way a helpful pharmacist speaks. Short sentences, one idea each.
- Use sentence case for headlines and captions. Keep ALL CAPS only for the small `caption` tags such as PRODUCT SPECS. The 2023 opening promo used loud caps and emoji; do not carry that over.
- Reuse the page's own lines: "We provide Affordable and Reliable Medical Equipment for all your Home and Corporate Needs", "The one-stop medical shop", "Reliable and Affordable Medical Supplies and Equipment", "Wholesale and Retail Available", "Call or Message us for all your medical supplies and equipment needs".
- Taglish is welcome in hashtags and short asides; the brand hashtag is #masmurakayemman. Keep headlines in English.
- No emoji, no stacked exclamation marks. One call to action per piece: Call or Message.
- State the sales channel on every product post: Wholesale and Retail.

## Visual foundations

- **Color.** Ground is `surface` (cream). Product windows and cards sit on `surface-raised`. Calm fields use `surface-mist` (sky) and `surface-sage`. `brand-sky` is the logo blue: use it for big shapes, discs and dividers only. Buttons and contact bars are `brand-deep` with `on-brand-deep` text. Blue text and links use `brand-ink`. Body text is `ink`, secondary text `ink-muted`.
- **Never** put white text on `brand-sky`, and never set text in `brand-sky` or `sage`. They are too light to read.
- **Type.** Headlines in the display family (`display-xl`, `display-lg`, `heading`, `subheading`), rounded and friendly. Everything else in `body`, `body-sm`, `caption`, `button`. Set the brand name as "Emmanuelle" light with "Pharma & Medical" bold when typing a lockup by hand.
- **Shape.** Everything is round. Cards use `radius-xl`, panels `radius-lg`, buttons and bars `radius-pill`. Photos sit inside a white disc or rounded window, never full bleed against a hard edge.
- **Space.** Leave air. Use `space-5` inside cards and `space-6` between sections. If a layout feels full, remove something.
- **Depth.** Only `shadow-soft`, and `shadow-lift` on hover. No borders for decoration; use `line` hairlines sparingly.
- **Motion.** Slow and small: 200 to 300ms fades. No bounce, no flashing.
- **Focus.** `focus` ring, 3px solid, 3px offset.
- **Dark theme.** "Calm night" keeps the same names; use it for screens used at night, not for print.

## Product post anatomy

Reflects the existing blue product cards on the page, softened:

1. `surface-mist` field with `radius-xl` corners.
2. Product name in `display-lg` or `subheading`, `ink`, top left.
3. Product photo inside a white disc (`surface-raised`), centered.
4. PRODUCT SPECS in `caption`, then three to five short spec lines in `body-sm`.
5. A `brand-deep` contact bar (`radius-pill`) with the number, and the email in `body-sm` below.
