Viber profile logos for Emmanuelle Pharma. Kept separate from the main logo files in `../`. All are 1024 x 1024 PNG, opaque, made for Viber's circular crop.

- `emmanuelle-viber-1024.png`: the E mark only, no name, on cream
- `emmanuelle-digital-sales-viber-1024.png`: E mark with a deep blue pill reading Digital Sales
- `emmanuelle-philgeps-viber-1024.png`: E mark with a deep blue pill reading PhilGEPS (text only, no government logo)

Layout for any new label logo:
- Cream ground #f6f4ee, 1024 x 1024
- E mark cropped from `../emmanuelle-stacked.png` (blue mark only, no text) at about 40% of the canvas height
- Pill 13% of the canvas high, 6% gap under the mark, pill fitted to the text with 6% padding each side
- Mark and pill are centered together as one block
- Pill fill brand-deep #0a6fa6, text white, Nunito ExtraBold (800), sentence case; never white text on brand sky
- No extra lines or taglines
- Check a circle crop before sending
