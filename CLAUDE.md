# Brand Media repo rules

- Every letterhead (brand level and government level, all brands) must follow [LETTERHEAD-SPEC.md](LETTERHEAD-SPEC.md). Zone A and Zone B on the first page header stay blank, the logo ends above y 350, and nothing sits under the logo.
- No em dashes in any publication copy, captions or files.
- Each brand lives in its own folder (medsource, emmanuelle-pharma, medplace) with brand/, posts/, print/ and a README.md.
