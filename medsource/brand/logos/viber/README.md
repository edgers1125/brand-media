Viber profile logos for MedSource and MedSource World. Kept separate from the main logo files in `../medsource/` and `../medsource-world/`. All are 1024 x 1024 PNG, opaque, made for Viber's circular crop (nothing sits near the edge).

- `medsource-viber-1024.png`: MedSource icon only, no name, on white
- `medsource-sta-lucia-viber-1024.png`: MedSource icon with a forest pill reading STA. LUCIA (branch)
- `medsource-san-mateo-viber-1024.png`: MedSource icon with a forest pill reading SAN MATEO (branch)
- `medsource-digital-sales-viber-1024.png`: MedSource icon with a forest pill reading DIGITAL SALES
- `medsource-world-philgeps-viber-1024.png`: MedSource World globe icon with a forest pill reading PhilGEPS (text only, no government logo)

Layout for any new label logo (a new branch or channel):
- White ground, 1024 x 1024
- Icon (from `../medsource/medsource-icon.png`, or `../medsource-world/world-icon.png` for MedSource World) at about 44% of the canvas height (40% for the globe), max 50% wide
- Pill 13% of the canvas high, 6% gap under the icon, pill width fitted to the text with 6% padding each side
- Icon and pill are centered together as one block, so the block sits in the middle of the circle
- Pill fill forest #445C44, text white, Poppins Bold, 0.6% tracking, all caps (PhilGEPS keeps its own casing); shrink the font if the pill goes wider than about 65% of the canvas
- No extra lines, taglines or the word MedSource under the pill
- Check a circle crop before sending
