# Publications — documents, social posts, print & signage

Every piece follows one look: white ground, straight full-width forest bands with white text, navy for weight, brand-green #7AB537 pills and stripes for the words that pop, Poppins only. The 2026 warehouse window sticker is the reference.

## The band system (all publications)

1. **Forest band** #445C44, full width, straight edges — the hero message in white Bold.
2. **Emphasis pill** — one word (Mura, ALL) in white Bold on a #7AB537 rounded pill. Once per piece.
3. **Brand-green stripe** — a thin #7AB537 rule between bands (about 1–1.5% of the height).
4. **White zone** — supporting line in navy, with a second phrase in forest ("Hospital-grade quality. / Abot-kayang presyo.").
5. **Navy band** #033F75 — sign-off: "Certified #Med**Mura**" or "Trust us, we've got it Medic**ALL**", with brand-green chevrons or arrows.
6. **Tiles** — rounded navy and forest tiles with white caps for product categories (MEDICAL DEVICES & EQUIPMENT · MEDICINES).
7. **Green block** — a #7AB537 block with navy-deep #022A4F caps for the call to action (VISIT US HERE →).

## Signage & window stickers

Two approved styles, both on the warehouse wall (3 panels: 35.24 × 44.84 in, 35.24 × 44.84 in, 16.57 × 44.84 in, read left to right as one wall: hook → what we have → call / visit, pointing to the entrance).

| Style | Look | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A · Full** | Every panel fully printed: logo on white, forest hero band, stripes, white zone, navy sign-off band; bands line up across all panels so the wall reads as one | Max impact, frosted-glass replacement |
| **B · Minimal band** | Clear glass everywhere except one forest band at eye level with a thin brand-green line under it. A white **logo tab** (full-colour logo) and a green **MedicALL tab** rise off the band's top edge. The band carries the hook, "from a certified ✓Wholesaler ✓Retailer ✓Importer", "Medical source for ALL your needs" with Devices · Supplies · Equipment · Medicines tiles, and the phone number | Clean, premium, keeps the windows open |

Rules for both:
- **Key content in the top ~70%** — the bottom third is hidden by planters, cars and people.
- Bands, stripes and baselines **continue across panels** at the same heights; design the wall, then cut it.
- **No QR codes, fine print or product photos** — they can't be read from the street. The phone number is the biggest thing on the last panel.
- The last panel always points toward the entrance (arrow) and says how to reach us (call / text, store hours).
- Big-format logos may be **rebalanced for signage only** (larger wordmark against the icon) — never for the official logo files.
- Print on white-backed vinyl so colours stay true on glass.

## Document page anatomy (from the 2025 profile)

1. **Logo** top-left (full colour on white, white on forest).
2. **Two-line title**: `title-light` "OUR" in ink → `title-bold` "SERVICES" in sage → a 90px rule in forest.
3. **Bands and photos**: a full-width forest band or a forest side panel with straight edges; photos may carry a diagonal cut, text never sits on one.
4. **Content**: `section-head` navy caps, or `label-bar` (navy bar, white caps), then justified `body`.
5. **Full-forest pages** (About, Get in touch) flip everything to white: white logo, white titles, white body, brand-green accents.
6. **Contact block**: navy bold "CONTACT US", icons in forest circles, details in ink.

## Social post anatomy

1. MedSource logo top-left, ~22% width.
2. One forest band carrying the headline in white, with the emphasis word in the green pill.
3. Two-line title in Poppins caps: "NEW / **ARRIVALS**", or one sentence-case headline for promos.
4. Product photo on a white ground.
5. Price in Poppins Bold navy; `promo-red` burst only for real sales.
6. Navy footer band with a thin brand-green stripe above: phone, branch, "Message us" pill.

## Do / Don't

- Don't use em dashes (—) in any publication copy, captions included. Use a period, comma, colon or line break instead.
- Do keep every band straight and full width; line bands up across panels and pages.
- Do use the green pill for one word only, and brand-green (not the older #99C55C) for every tagline accent.
- Do keep body text justified in documents, ragged in social.
- Don't put text on a slanted or diagonal background.
- Don't flood a page with brand-green — pills, stripes and small blocks only.
- Don't use small white text on brand-green; use navy-deep instead.
- Don't put the full-colour logo on forest, navy or photos — use the white version.
- Don't mix more than Poppins + one optional Canva display face in a piece.
