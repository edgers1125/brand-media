# MedSource — brand identity

One identity for two names:
- **MedSource World** — the parent, legal company: importation, procurement and distribution for hospitals, LGUs, government agencies and corporations (8,000+ products, 200+ hospitals, LGUs and agencies served).
- **MedSource Medical Supplies & Equipment** — the retail face: the "all-in-one medical outlet" for families, students and clinics, bringing accessible, dependable and trusted supplies to the heart of the Filipino people.

Same banana: same colours, same type, same layouts. Only the logo changes with the audience. This system is the **official reference for every publication** — company profiles, proposals, social posts, promos, price lists, signage.

## The feel

Set by the 2025 Company Profile and refined on the 2026 warehouse window sticker (the approved reference):
- **Corporate, calm, trustworthy — and unmistakably green.** White grounds, deep forest-green bands, navy for weight, bright brand-green #7AB537 for the words that must pop.
- **Straight, full-width bands.** Text always sits on straight bands — never on slanted backgrounds (straight text on a slant reads as a mistake). Diagonal cuts are kept for photos and decorative corners only, never behind type.
- **The green pill.** The one emphasis word — **Mura**, **ALL** — sits in a #7AB537 rounded pill with white Bold text. This is the signature move; use it once per piece.
- **Thin #7AB537 stripes** separate bands (a "rule" between forest, white and navy).
- **Big two-line caps titles** — "OUR / **SERVICES**" — a light line, then a bold line, then a short rule.
- **Navy label bars and tiles** with white caps for product categories, service names and VISION / MISSION.
- **Real photography** of stock and equipment where a layout needs it.

## Logos

The **original logos are kept** — the lighter blue box under the green cross gives the mark its contrast. They are now clean vector files (the icon used to be an embedded picture), and the tagline "MEDICAL SUPPLIES & EQUIPMENT" spans the full width of the wordmark so it sits with it instead of trailing off.

**Logo-to-text ratio (rebalanced 2026-10-06).** The name was too small next to the icon, so every lockup was rebuilt with the same icon and a larger wordmark:
- **Landscape** (MedSource and MedSource World): the wordmark block (name + tagline / WORLD) is about **2/3 of the icon height**, centred on the icon, with a gap of about 1/8 of the icon height.
- **Portrait** (stacked): the wordmark is **2× the icon width**, centred under the icon.
- Icon-only files are unchanged.

| Use | Logo |
| --- | --- |
| Company profile, proposals, B2B, government, letterheads | **MedSource World** — globe + cross; MED (green) SOURCE (navy) / WORLD in Montserrat |
| Retail store, social posts, promos, price lists | **MedSource** — cross + box; Med (navy) Source (green) in Poppins |

Full colour on white or cream; **white versions on forest, navy or photos**. Clear space = height of the cap letters; never recolour, stretch or retype. Logo colours belong to the logo only — layouts use the publication palette below.

**Logo colours** (fixed inside the marks — don't use them for layouts): navy #033F75, Source green #62A40B, cross gradient #AFD176 → #66AB1E, box gradient #5798BF → #256C90, tagline grey #646464.

**Proportion: 45 white · 30 forest · 15 navy · 10 brand-green.** Forest leads so the brand reads green; navy anchors; brand-green is the accent that pops. Brand-green fills only pills, stripes and small blocks — never a whole page.

Readability (checked): white on forest 7.2:1, on navy 10.6:1, on sage 5.2:1; navy-deep on brand-green 5.9:1; brand-green on navy 4.6:1; ink on brand-green 7.2:1. **White on brand-green is 2.4:1 — allowed only for the big Bold pill word (Mura, ALL) at display size**; small white labels on green use `brand-green-strong` #4A7F08 instead.

## Typography

**Poppins is the house face** — the MedSource wordmark, all titles, all text.
- Titles: ALL CAPS, two lines — `title-light` (Regular, ink/white) then `title-bold` (Bold, sage/white), then a short rule.
- `section-head`: Bold caps, navy. `label-bar`: Bold caps, white on a navy bar.
- `lead` and `body`: Regular, sentence case; body justified in documents.
- Montserrat stays inside the MedSource World logo only — never for text. Canva extras (Cy Grotesk, Public Sans, Open Sauce) are optional for social only; Poppins alone covers everything.

## Taglines

- **Main — "#MedMura"**: hero version on a forest band — "**#Med**" Bold white + "**Mura**" Bold white inside a brand-green pill. Sign-off version "Certified #MedMura" on navy — "Certified #Med" white, "**Mura**" brand-green. Hook line: "Gusto mo ba ng #MedMura?"
- **Secondary — "Trust us, we've got it MedicALL"**: "Trust us, we've got it" small, Medium; "**MedicALL**" much larger, Bold. On navy: "Medic" brand-green, "**ALL**" white. In a brand-green speech bubble: "Medic" white, "**ALL**" navy-deep. In copy: "medical source for **ALL** your medical needs" with ALL in the green pill.
- Spelling fixed: **#MedMura**, **MedicALL**, "we've got it". Best on forest or navy bands; on white, set "Mura"/"Medic" large only.

## Voice

- **MedSource World:** formal, assured, institutional — "reliable", "professional-grade", "end-to-end", "nationwide".
- **MedSource retail:** warm and helpful, Taglish welcome, always clear on product, price and how to buy; max two emoji per caption.
- Write "MedSource" and "MedSource World" — one word, capital M and S.

## Using this system

Tokens have a light theme (print, profile, default) and a dark theme. Spacing is an 8px grid. Shapes are **straight full-width bands** separated by thin brand-green stripes; rounded corners are for the emphasis pill, buttons, chips, tiles and product cards. Diagonals only on photos or decoration, never behind text. See **Publications** for page and post layouts and **Decisions** for what was settled.
