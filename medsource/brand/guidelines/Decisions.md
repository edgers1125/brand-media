# Decisions

## Logo — original kept
- The original MedSource and MedSource World logos stay, colours and lettering unchanged: the lighter blue box (#5798BF → #256C90) keeps the contrast against the green cross.
- Rebuilt as **clean vector files** — the icon used to be an embedded picture, which is what showed up broken.
- The tagline **"MEDICAL SUPPLIES & EQUIPMENT" now spans the full wordmark width** in both lockups, so it reads as one unit instead of trailing off.
- The MedSource World logo's icon is redrawn from a small 229×240 px original; have a designer check it before large print.

## 2026-10-05 — Sticker look becomes the house style
- Text sits on **straight full-width bands** only; slanted backgrounds behind text are dropped (diagonals stay for photos/decoration).
- **Brand-green #7AB537 promoted** to the pop colour: emphasis pills (Mura, ALL) with white Bold text, stripes between bands, tagline accents. The softer mura-green #99C55C is legacy.
- Forest leads the proportion (45 white · 30 forest · 15 navy · 10 brand-green) so the brand reads green, not blue.
- Signage gets two approved styles: **A · Full** and **B · Minimal band**, with visibility rules (key content in the top 70%, no QR/fine print, phone number biggest on the last panel).


## 2026-10-06 — Logo-to-text ratio rebalanced
- All MedSource and MedSource World lockups (colour and white) rebuilt: same icon, bigger wordmark.
- Landscape: wordmark block ≈ 2/3 of icon height (was ≈ 1/2), centred on the icon.
- Portrait: wordmark ≈ 2× icon width (was ≈ 1.6×).
- Icons alone unchanged. These replace the previous files everywhere.
