# HealthHub by MedSource

Healthcare brand of MedSource HealthHub (sister company of MedSource), formerly drafted as MedHealth. Mark: cross with heartbeat. Colours: navy #033F75, green #5FA122, grey #646464, gradient blue #4AA7D6 to #0757A8 and green #A5DB6B to #4FA547. Fonts: Poppins (wordmark), Quicksand (tagline). Tagline: Advancing Healthcare Solutions. Descriptor: Diagnostics and Holistic Wellness.

Letterhead format: [LETTERHEAD-SPEC.md](../LETTERHEAD-SPEC.md). No em dashes.
