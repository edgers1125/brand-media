Government level letterhead for HealthHub by MedSource. Same look as the [brand letterhead](../2026-10-healthhub-letterhead/print.md), with the business address (209 Riverside Drive, Provident Village, Marikina City) added to the first page header under the tagline and green rule. No contact person or phone. The later pages header and the footer are the same as the brand letterhead.

Like the other government letterheads, the first page header prints the address in the right hand block, so the Zone B area of [LETTERHEAD-SPEC.md](../../../LETTERHEAD-SPEC.md) is used on purpose here. Zone A stays blank, and the logo ends at x 1235 and y 291.
