EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A one-stop community healthcare hub

A YAKAP Center and YAKAP-Gamot claiming center at Sta. Lucia Mall.

Problem

PhilHealth YAKAP members struggle to reach YAKAP centers and claim their prescribed medicines efficiently, delaying the continuation of their care.

Our solution

Through M.D. Source, MedSource HealthHub will open a YAKAP Center and an exclusive YAKAP-Gamot claiming center, and run medical caravans for easier, community-based medicine claiming.

Value proposition

Preventive care, early diagnosis and seamless access to government health benefits, closer to the community, creating value for patients, employers, local governments and partners like Sta. Lucia Mall.
