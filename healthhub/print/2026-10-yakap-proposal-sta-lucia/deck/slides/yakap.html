THE PROGRAM

The YAKAP program

PhilHealth YAKAP stands for Yaman ng Kalusugan Program: the government’s expanded primary care and outpatient program that gives Filipinos easier access to consultations, laboratory tests, preventive care and medicines through accredited YAKAP clinics and Gamot Providers.

YAKAP Clinic

Deliver accessible, comprehensive and continuous primary care with proper patient monitoring, preventive care and referral support.

Maintain PhilHealth-accredited standards through quality assurance, EMR compliance, operational readiness and authorized personnel.

YAKAP Gamot

Dispense PhilHealth-covered medications to eligible YAKAP members with proper prescription validation and continuity of treatment.

Maintain adequate medicine inventory and comply with PhilHealth documentation, reimbursement and system requirements.
