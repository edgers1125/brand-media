OUR BRAND

M.D. Source Diagnostics and Holistic Wellness is the brand of MedSource HealthHub Inc.: a PhilHealth YAKAP primary care clinic and diagnostic center for diagnostics, consultations, YAKAP Gamot and DSWD assistance, and government healthcare programs in one convenient place.

Background

Established to bridge the gap between healthcare services and the communities that need them most, making quality care accessible, affordable and patient-centered for every Filipino.

Vision

To be the most trusted neighborhood health hub for Filipino families, where primary care, government health benefits, medicines and diagnostics come together under one roof.

Mission

To give Filipino families one trusted place, to access primary medical care, medicines and diagnostics all in one simple process, so getting care is simple, affordable and close to home.

M.D. Source Diagnostics and Holistic Wellness is the brand of MedSource HealthHub Inc., the healthcare company of the MedSource family. Through integrated medical services and PhilHealth-supported programs, it makes quality healthcare more accessible, affordable and patient-centered.
