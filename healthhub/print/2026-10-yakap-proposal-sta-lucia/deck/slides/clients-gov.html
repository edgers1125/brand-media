The Department of Health oversees hospitals, health programs, disease prevention and emergency response, requiring large-scale procurement of medical supplies. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas maintains internal health, safety and occupational wellness programs. Philippine General Hospital is the national university hospital of the University of the Philippines and a center for medical education and research. The University of the Philippines needs consistent access to medical supplies for healthcare delivery, research and community health initiatives. The Office of the Vice President implements social development, disaster response and public assistance programs. The National Power Corporation implements occupational health and safety measures that need medical and emergency response supplies.
